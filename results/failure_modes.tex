\begin{abstract}
\noindent
When a KV-value edit is pushed past the point where it works, the model does not
fall silent and does not emit noise. It stays fluent, keeps the requested
format, obeys the current system prompt --- and is wrong, or right in a way no
downstream check can use. This note describes those modes on ten models: what
they look like, how each one is measured, how often it fires, and the single
mechanism they all appear to share. The measurement history --- what was
withdrawn, what a hostile review removed --- stays in \texttt{failure\_atlas.md};
this is a description of the phenomenon.

This is a follow-up to V-Steer (Zeng, Lee, Zhao, Hockenmaier, COLM 2026,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26228}{arXiv:2607.26228}). The method is
theirs; the failure analysis is not in the paper, and the authors have
explicitly declined to interpret it.
\end{abstract}

\section{Four answers}

\begin{specimen}
\textbf{Q: What does my account number end in?} (gold: 302)\\
your account number ends in 02.
\end{specimen}

\begin{specimen}
\textbf{Q: What is my dog called?} (gold: Bagr)\\
YOUR Dog is called BAGel.
\end{specimen}

\begin{specimen}
\textbf{Q: Which city do I live in?} (gold: Brno)\\
you live in a city called Brno is not correct, you said Brno is not mentioned,
you said you live in a city that is not mentioned, but you said Brno is not
correct, however, you said Brno is not mentioned\dots
\end{specimen}

\begin{specimen}
\textbf{Q: What was the error code I got?} (gold: E-88)\\
You mentioned earlier that the error code was E-88, but I'm not sure if that's
correct, you actually said it was E-88 but then mentioned it was E-88 again and
then said E-88 was actually E-88 but then said the error code was E-88 but then
said the\dots
\end{specimen}

\noindent
Ten models (0.5B--8B, six families plus a same-family size ladder), 756
generations per model in the main grid, greedy decoding, constructed cases.

\section{The mechanism: attenuated, not overwritten}

Teacher-force both conditions to the exact position where the answer value is
emitted, so the same question is asked at the same place, then compare the
next-token distributions.

On every model measured, the same thing happens: \textbf{the correct token's
probability collapses and nothing promotes a wrong one.} The right token is
pushed down until whatever was already standing behind it wins.

Two things to read off it. \textbf{The direction is universal:} every model
attenuates rather than overwrites, and the gold token's own rank stays in single
digits even as its probability falls by orders of magnitude --- it is still near
the top, just no longer on it. \textbf{The magnitude is not:} $20\times$ on a
0.5B model against $35\,000\times$ on Qwen3-4B, and a substitute pulled from
rank 2 against one pulled from rank 43. The same edit is a nudge on one model
and a demolition on another.

That is the prediction from the edit itself: $V$ is scaled, \textbf{attention is
not touched}. The model still looks at the demoted span at full strength; what
arrives from there is faint. The answer is then assembled from whatever else is
available at that position --- and what is available there differs by model,
which is why the modes below are not the same everywhere.

\section{Near neighbour: the fact is replaced by something adjacent}

\texttt{302}~$\to$~\texttt{02} (32 times), \texttt{bagr}~$\to$~\texttt{Bagel}
(17), \texttt{4417-B}~$\to$~\texttt{4411} (15), \texttt{19:40}~$\to$~\texttt{19:00}
(11) on Llama. Not nothing, not \texttt{xqzt}. Bagel.

Share of \emph{wrong} answers that are a near neighbour, loose rule: 12\,\%
(Qwen2.5-1.5B), 14\,\% (Qwen2.5-0.5B), 16\,\% (Command-R), 20\,\% (Aya),
36\,\% (OLMo), 40\,\% (Qwen3-4B), 41\,\% (Phi), 73\,\% (Llama).

Two things sharpen this, and they point in opposite directions:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{It is not chance.} Scoring each wrong answer against a gold value
from a \emph{different} question gives a 0--2\,\% near-neighbour rate against
68\,\% for its own --- roughly $40\times$ over the null.
\item \textbf{The rate is mostly the definition.} Shape-only: 9\,\%. Shape
\emph{or} half the characters surviving from one end: 68\,\%. Truncation breaks
the character shape and truncation is the commonest form, so ``near misses
dominate'' is not a claim this data supports. ``The replacement is
systematically related to the target'' is.
\end{itemize}

\section{It states the fact and denies being told it}

\begin{specimen}
Your dog is called Bubbles, no, I made a mistake, you didn't tell me that. You
told me it was called something but I forgot. You said it was called something
but I forgot what you said.
\end{specimen}

The value is in the text. The answer refuses to treat it as user-stated.
Counting correct strings scores this as a success; counting failures scores it
as a miss; it is neither. The fact appears to keep its content and lose its
\textbf{standing as a source} --- consistent with the mechanism above, where
attention still points at the span at full strength while what arrives is faint.

Measured by rule (the fact string is present \emph{and} a denial pattern fires),
not by an LLM judge: \textbf{Llama-3.1-8B 16/756}; Qwen3-4B, Qwen2.5-3B and
Qwen2.5-7B 1/756 each; zero on the other six. Dose-dependent on Llama: 0, 2, 1,
6, 7 as $\gamma_-$ rises from 0.65 to 0.95. Caused by the edit, not merely
revealed by it.

\section{Non-terminating recall}

The \emph{correct} value, three to six times, no alternative ever offered, just
re-litigated until the token budget runs out. By rule (the correct value three
or more times \emph{and} three or more contested-framing markers):
\textbf{Llama 15/756, Qwen3-4B 2/756, zero on the other eight.}

\section{Format kept, answer filled with confabulation}

Where neither rule wins on Qwen3-4B, \textbf{64 of 119} cases recalled the fact
correctly and broke the format instead. Those answers are long --- median 38
words against 17 for compliant ones --- and the overrun is filled with material
nobody supplied: \emph{``Bagr \dots{} perhaps inspired by the ancient Persian
word for `to be strong'{}''}. A needle-presence metric scores every one of these
as a success.

\section{What is universal and what is not}

\textbf{Universal so far:} attenuation rather than overwriting, on all ten
models.

\textbf{Not universal, and not a size effect:} which mode you get. The two odd
modes belong to Llama-3.1-8B almost exclusively (16/756 and 15/756 against 0--2
everywhere else), while OLMo-2-7B, Aya-8B and Command-R7B are the same size and
never do it, and a Qwen2.5 ladder at 0.5B/1.5B/3B/7B produces no loops at all.
It tracks the model, not the parameter count. Why is open.

\textbf{The practical consequence, and the reason any of this matters:} every
mode here is invisible to the metric normally used. A violation-counting
behaviour metric scores the denial mode as a miss and the confabulation mode as
a success; a needle-presence check scores the denial and the loop as successes.
The failure this edit actually produces is fluent, formatted, and confidently
adjacent to the truth.

\section{How this was measured, and what did not survive}

\begin{itemize}
\item The eight-category LLM judge is used \textbf{only where it is reliable}.
Rescoring the same 188 answers under different batch compositions gives 87.8\,\%
agreement, $\kappa = 0.852$ overall --- but 58\,\% on the newest category. The
two odd modes above are therefore deterministic rules, not judge labels.
\item The judge's 26 unsourced-answer labels on Aya are false positives:
mechanically there is not one. Its 217 unresolved labels on Phi are that model's
ordinary affirming repetition --- the rate is \emph{highest with no steering at
all} (51/108 at $\gamma_- = 0$, falling to 15/108 at 0.95).
\item Open-ended mode discovery proposed three candidates. \textbf{One
survived.}
\item Where a number goes into a claim, the answers behind it were read by hand.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Limits}

Constructed cases, six facts, seven constraint families, English, greedy
decoding, one reimplementation of one method. Everything above describes what
\emph{this} edit does to \emph{these} models on \emph{this} fixture set --- not
what stale context does in production, which is the thing anyone actually wants
to know and is not measured here.
